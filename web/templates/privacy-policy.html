Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

Information We Collect

When you use TraceQube tools, we process the input you provide (IP addresses, domain names, URLs) to perform the requested diagnostic. We do not store this input data beyond the request.

When you create an API account, we collect your email address and store a hashed version of your password. We collect usage counts for rate limiting purposes.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand aggregate usage patterns. This collects anonymized data including pages visited, browser type, and approximate geographic region. No personally identifiable information is collected.

Advertising

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on the free tools. Google may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share your data with third parties except as required by law or as necessary to operate the service (e.g. payment processing via PayPal).

Cookies

We store a theme preference (dark/light mode) in your browser's local storage. Analytics and advertising providers may set cookies per their own privacy policies.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact us at info@traceqube.com
