What Is My IP Address?

Your IP address (Internet Protocol address) is a unique numerical label assigned to your device by your ISP. It identifies your device on the network and provides your approximate location to servers you visit.

What Does My IP Reveal?

Your public IP can reveal your approximate location (country, region, city), your Internet Service Provider, your Autonomous System Number (ASN), and your timezone. It does not reveal your exact address or personal details.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does my IP change? Most residential ISPs assign dynamic IPs that change periodically. Businesses often use static IPs.

Can websites see my IP? Yes — every website you visit can see your public IP. This is how responses are routed back to your device.

How do I hide my IP? Use a VPN or proxy server to route traffic through a different IP address.
