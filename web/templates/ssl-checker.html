SSL Certificate Checker

Verify SSL certificate validity, expiry date, issuer, and TLS version.

What Is an SSL Certificate?

An SSL/TLS certificate authenticates a website's identity and enables encrypted HTTPS connections. Expired or invalid certificates trigger browser security warnings and can hurt SEO rankings.

What to Check

Expiry dateCertificates expire after 90 days (Let's Encrypt) or up to 1 year.
IssuerThe Certificate Authority that issued the cert.
TLS versionTLSv1.3 is the current standard. Avoid TLSv1.0 and TLSv1.1.

Common Use Cases

  • Expiry monitoring — catch certificates approaching expiration before they cause browser warnings or outages.
  • Chain validation — confirm intermediate certificates are properly configured, since missing links cause errors on some browsers/devices even when the cert appears valid on others.
  • Post-renewal verification — confirm a newly renewed or reissued certificate deployed correctly after an update.
  • Third-party/vendor checks — verify the SSL configuration of an API or service you integrate with before troubleshooting connection failures.

Example

A certificate expiring in 12 days with a valid chain and strong TLS 1.3 support is healthy today but needs renewal scheduled now — most outages from expired certs are avoidable with a 30-day expiry alert.

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens when an SSL certificate expires?

Browsers will show a security warning and typically block users from proceeding, effectively taking your site offline for most visitors until it's renewed.

What is a certificate chain issue?

It means the server isn't sending the full chain of intermediate certificates needed for browsers to verify trust up to a recognized root authority — a very common misconfiguration.

Is a self-signed certificate a problem?

For public-facing production sites, yes — browsers won't trust it and will show warnings. Self-signed certs are fine for internal/development use only.

How often should I renew my SSL certificate?

Most modern certificates (like Let's Encrypt) are valid for 90 days and are usually configured to auto-renew. Manually issued certificates can be valid for up to a year or more.
